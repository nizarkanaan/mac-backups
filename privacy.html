Mac Backups

Privacy policy

This policy describes the intended operation of this single-user backup configuration.

Backup data

Selected files and their metadata are processed locally by restic. Backup contents and file metadata are encrypted on the Mac before upload. The encrypted repository is stored in the owner’s Google Drive. Google processes account, storage, and service-request information under its own policies.

Google Drive permission

The rclone connection uses drive.file. In this configuration it manages files and folders created through that connection for backups, rather than requesting access to all existing Drive files.

Credentials and access

OAuth credentials, access tokens, and backup settings are stored locally in private configuration files. They are not published on this website. The owner controls the backup encryption password and can revoke the connection in Google Account settings.

Retention and deletion

The owner chooses backup retention and manages deletion of backup data in Google Drive. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing encrypted backups.

This website

These static pages contain no forms, advertising, analytics code, or application cookies. They do not receive Google credentials, backup files, or encryption passwords. The hosting provider may process normal website-request logs under its own policies.

Questions

This configuration is for its owner’s personal use. The support contact is shown on its Google OAuth consent screen.
